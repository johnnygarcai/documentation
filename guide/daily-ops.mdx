Most of the program runs itself. These are the parts that need a person, roughly in priority order.

## 1. The post review queue

**Social tab, defaults to pending.** Posts count toward bonus floors and bounties once approved, and the funnel reads zero for a creator until their posts clear. Nothing is ever lost (credit applies retroactively by post date), but the numbers lag until the queue is worked, and creators watching their own progress feel that lag directly.

Approving is one click; denying asks for a reason, which the creator sees. If a post is borderline, deny with a kind, specific reason rather than letting it sit.

## 2. Action items

**14-Day Sprint tab, section 01.** Room adds and removals, each with a Mark done checkoff so the work isn't duplicated. The **Copy usernames** button grabs every outstanding handle for bulk invites.

The two bonus worklists sit underneath:

- **\$40 Get Set Up bonus**: paid manually, so the Mark paid button is only the double-payment guard. Check it the moment the payment is sent.
- **\$20 curriculum bonus**: Create payout writes a real pending row into the ledger, which then goes through normal approval.

## 3. Outreach to the quiet ones

Two lists tell you who needs a personal nudge:

- **The reactivation panel** (waitlist section): every reactivated creator with a "came back" timestamp. The rows with no signal yet are your outreach list.
- **The Official tab worklists**: Idle (no post in 7 days) and Needs CS Room invite, both with checkoffs.

## 4. Room approvals

The **Joiners roster** shows everyone who applied with an In room / Not yet pill. Use it to verify sprint-room access requests: green means they're already in. Blocked creators are flagged so they don't get approved into the room.

<Warning>
  Approving payouts and sending money are operator-only actions and stay that way. If you need a payout approved, flag it to an operator rather than working around the gate.
</Warning>

## When something looks wrong

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the cohort selector">
    The number you're looking at is scoped to the selected cohort. This explains most surprises.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the review queue">
    A dead-looking posts stage usually means pending review \+ warm-up windows, not inactive creators. The posting activity strip under the funnel shows the truth.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check verification health">
    Rising unverified verdicts usually mean verification is failing, not creators.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Then ask">
    If it still looks wrong, screenshot it and post in the team channel. Two of the biggest catches this month started with someone saying "this looks off."
  </Step>
</Steps>
