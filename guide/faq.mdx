<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The funnel numbers look wrong">
    In order: check the cohort selector (this is the answer most of the time), check whether you're comparing across program versions (older cohorts have different stages), check the posting activity strip (pending review \+ warm-up windows make a busy cohort read as zero), and check verification health (verification failing shows up as creators failing).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A creator says they finished but the dashboard disagrees">
    Open them in the roster and read the per-requirement detail rather than the verdict. For curriculum questions there's a per-video support view, and staff can credit a video by hand when a player never reported it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why is a creator Unverified?">
    Unverified means something couldn't be confirmed, which is different from the creator not doing the work. Verification health is the first place to look, then their roster row.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What's the difference between the two bonus worklists?">
    The **\$40 Get Set Up bonus** is paid manually, so Mark paid is just the double-payment guard. The **\$20 curriculum bonus** creates a real pending payout that goes through normal approval. One is bookkeeping, the other moves money.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is Cohort 8R?">
    The reactivated waitlist group: 485 people who applied earlier, sat on the waitlist, and were enrolled together on August 4 with the standard welcome. They live in their own parallel cohort so cohort 8's ad-test numbers stay clean, and they show violet in the applicants chart and a batch chip in the reactivation panel.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why do some creators show a WAITLIST chip?">
    They hit a full enrollment cap before their slot opened, back when caps existed. It marks history, and it's how the from-waitlist filter keeps ad-performance reads clean.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I share preview links outside the team?">
    Yes. The public previews page runs on mock data with no login and is safe to hand to ad creators. The dashboard itself shows real creator emails and payout amounts, so screenshots of it stay internal.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="When do posting bonuses actually count?">
    A post counts toward floors and bounties once it's approved in the review queue, and it credits back to its posting date. So a slow review queue never costs a creator their bonus, it just delays the numbers.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A legacy (affiliate network) creator is applying to the sprint. Approve them?">
    Hold off for now. Legacy creators go through the official migration coming in August, which brings their history, characters, and tier into our program properly. Approving them into the sprint room early creates a split record. If their Cantina username is known to be active in the affiliate network, deny the room request kindly and let them know the migration is coming. When in doubt, ask in the team channel with their Cantina username.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can a creator reuse an existing TikTok or Instagram account?">
    The rule from the 8/5 team call: if the account was made organically for Cantina content and they were never part of the legacy affiliate-network program, allow it. We don't audit account ages one by one. If they ARE a legacy creator, their accounts wait for the migration instead.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What's the Big Buck Bunny video in the previews?">
    A deliberate placeholder. Explainer-video slots that don't have a real video yet show the Bunny on preview pages only (live creator surfaces show nothing until a real video ships). The pigeon Get Set Up video is the current real one, and the vision is a short explainer for every part of the portal. Preview stand-ins are now labeled as placeholders on the video itself.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Who do I escalate money questions to?">
    Operators. Payout approval and sending are operator-gated, and disputed amounts should go to the team channel with the creator's email and the payout row's status.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
