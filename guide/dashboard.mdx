The admin dashboard lives at `joincharacterstudio.com/dashboard`, behind the Cantina Google sign-in. The page titles itself **Admin Panel**; the **14-Day Sprint** tab is the one you'll live in.

<Frame caption="The dashboard header and tab strip">
  ![Dashboard tabs](https://www.joincharacterstudio.com/docs/dash-tabs.png)
</Frame>

## The five tabs

| Tab | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **14-Day Sprint** | The default. Cohort overview, funnel, verdicts, the roster, payouts, and the daily worklists. |
| **Official Program** | Graduates on the ongoing program: their roster, payout review, and ledger. |
| **Social** | Program-wide posts and impressions, plus the post review queue. |
| **Site previews** | One-click links into every creator-facing screen, with mock data. |
| **Community** | The forum moderation queue. |

## The one concept that explains most numbers

**Almost everything is scoped to the selected cohort.** The dropdown at the top scopes nearly every number below it, so a funnel that looks wrong is very often the right funnel for the wrong cohort. The dashboard snaps to the newest launch cohort on load; audience tests (including Cohort 8R, the reactivated waitlist group) are grouped separately in the dropdown. See [The cohorts right now](/program/cohorts) for what each one is.

## Section by section

### Cohort overview

The selector, the headline tiles (signups, downloads, room joins, waitlist history, reactivated), and the **Applicants by day** chart: new enrollments (teal), reactivated waitlisters (violet), and historical cap-full waitlist signups (amber) stacked per day, with the enrollment state pinned beside it.

### Action items

The daily worklist: room adds and removals with checkoffs so two people never do the same job twice, plus the two bonus worklists ($$40 Get Set Up, $$20 curriculum). Covered in [Daily ops](/guide/daily-ops).

### Funnel and posting activity

The funnel shows the share of cohort signups reaching each stage, from signup through graduation. Directly under it, the **posting activity strip** shows the raw layer behind the posts stage: who's posting, how many posts are pending review, and how many are countable right now.

### Funnel trends

The same funnel over time: week-over-week and month-over-month movement, for the question the snapshot can't answer (is this getting better or worse).

### Verdicts and requirements

The cohort split by outcome (pass, in progress, unverified, fail) plus per-requirement completion, which is how you find the step people get stuck on. A **PEND** badge means that number isn't fully trusted yet.

<Frame caption="Verdict distribution and requirement completion">
  ![Verdicts](https://www.joincharacterstudio.com/docs/program-04-verdicts.png)
</Frame>

### In-app activity

What creators did inside the Cantina app (characters, videos, rooms), from the daily passback. It moves on the upstream feed's schedule, so a flat number here is worth a second look before treating it as a real drop.

<Frame caption="In-app activity tiles">
  ![In-app activity](https://www.joincharacterstudio.com/docs/program-05-in-app.png)
</Frame>

### Verification health

Whether social verification is actually working. This is the first stop when unverified verdicts climb, because the usual cause is verification failing rather than creators failing.

<Frame caption="Verification health">
  ![Verification health](https://www.joincharacterstudio.com/docs/program-07-verification.png)
</Frame>

### Joiners, payouts, and engagement

**Joiners** is the roster: every applicant with handles, progress, outreach state, and a per-creator detail view; search reaches across cohorts after two characters. **Payouts** is the ledger plus duplicate-account signals worth clearing before approvals. **Email engagement** and **Portal activity** show whether comms land and what creators do in their workspace.

<Frame caption="Portal activity: identified creators and the stages they reach">
  ![Portal activity](https://www.joincharacterstudio.com/docs/program-11-portal-activity.png)

### Site previews

Every creator-facing screen as one-click preview links with mock data, grouped by program version. The same list is public at `joincharacterstudio.com/previews` for sharing with ad creators.
