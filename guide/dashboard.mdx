The admin dashboard lives at `joincharacterstudio.com/dashboard`, behind the Cantina Google sign-in. The page titles itself **Admin Panel**; the **14-Day Sprint** tab is the one you'll live in.

<Frame caption="The dashboard header and tab strip">

</Frame>

## The five tabs

| Tab | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **14-Day Sprint** | The default. Cohort overview, funnel, verdicts, the roster, payouts, and the daily worklists. |
| **Official Program** | Graduates on the ongoing program: their roster, payout review, and ledger. |
| **Social** | Program-wide posts and impressions, plus the post review queue. |
| **Site previews** | One-click links into every creator-facing screen, with mock data. |
| **Community** | The forum moderation queue. |

## The one concept that explains most numbers

**Almost everything is scoped to the selected cohort.** The dropdown at the top scopes nearly every number below it, so a funnel that looks wrong is very often the right funnel for the wrong cohort. The dashboard snaps to the newest launch cohort on load; audience tests (including Cohort 8R, the reactivated waitlist group) are grouped separately in the dropdown.

## Key sections on the 14-Day Sprint tab

**Cohort overview** sits at the top: the selector, headline tiles (signups, downloads, room joins, waitlist history, reactivated), and the **Applicants by day** chart. The chart stacks new enrollments (teal), reactivated waitlisters (violet), and historical cap-full waitlist signups (amber), and the enrollment chip shows the open/no-cap state at a glance.

<Frame caption="Applicants by day, with the reactivation split">
  ![Applicants by day chart](https://intent-tortoise-231.convex.cloud/api/storage/4b2bc078-21a8-437b-8c5f-f0d3be90fb45)

**Action items** is the daily worklist: room adds/removals with checkoffs so two people never do the same job twice, plus the two bonus worklists ($$40 Get Set Up, $$20 curriculum).

**The funnel** shows the share of cohort signups reaching each stage, from signup through graduation. Directly under it, the **posting activity strip** shows the raw layer behind the posts stage: who's posting, how many posts are pending review, and how many are countable right now.

<Frame caption="Posting activity behind the funnel's posts stage">
  ![Posting activity strip](https://intent-tortoise-231.convex.cloud/api/storage/82d74e42-9cc4-43c2-ad9e-33992a14122f)
</Frame>

**Verdicts and requirements** splits the cohort by outcome (pass, in progress, unverified, fail) and shows per-requirement completion, which is how you find the step people get stuck on. A **PEND** badge means that number isn't fully trusted yet.

<Frame caption="Verdict distribution and requirement completion">
  ![Verdicts](https://www.joincharacterstudio.com/docs/program-04-verdicts.png)
</Frame>

**Joiners** is the roster: every applicant with their handles, progress, outreach state, and a detail view per creator. Search reaches across cohorts once you type two characters.

**Payouts** is the ledger plus duplicate-account signals worth clearing before approvals. **Email engagement** and **Portal activity** show whether comms land and what creators do in their workspace.
