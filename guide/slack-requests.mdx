Anything you can't resolve yourself in the dashboard goes through the team Slack channel. A good request gets resolved in one pass; a vague one turns into a question thread. This page is the difference.

## The golden rule

**One creator, one message, all the context.** Every request should carry:

1. **The creator's email** (the one on their application; it's the key to every record)
2. **What they expected vs what happened**, in one sentence each
3. **A screenshot** of what you're seeing (dashboard row, portal state, or their DM)
4. **What you already checked**, so nobody re-walks the same ground

<Tip>
  The creator detail modal in the dashboard roster is the best screenshot for most requests: it shows their checklist, timestamps, and verdict in one frame.
</Tip>

## Request types and what each needs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Payout question or correction">
    Include the creator email, which payout (the \$40, the \$20 curriculum, a weekly bounty, or per-post pay), and the status you see in the ledger. Money changes are operator-only and audit-logged, so these always route through Slack rather than being handled in DMs.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Curriculum credit">
    Creator email plus which video didn't credit. Staff can credit it manually in about a minute, and the fix is logged with a reason.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Room access">
    Creator email and their Cantina username. Check the roster's In-room pill first: if it's already green, the answer is usually app-side, not ours.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Verification stuck">
    Creator email, the handle they're verifying, and whether the code is currently in their bio (a screenshot of the bio helps). If Verification health shows program-wide failures, say so: that's a different, more urgent report.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Wrong or changed email">
    The old email, the new email, and how the creator proved they own both. Email is the identity key for their whole record, so this is deliberately a careful, operator-handled change.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Something looks broken">
    A screenshot, the URL you were on, and what you expected. "This looks off" with a screenshot has caught real problems within hours; never assume someone else already noticed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Idea or feature request">
    Welcome, genuinely. Describe the situation that made you want it (the story is more useful than the feature), and it goes on the build list with your name on it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## What happens after you post

Requests get picked up by whoever owns that area (payouts by operators, program calls by the leads, build issues by engineering). You'll get a thread reply when it's done or if something's needed from you. If a creator is waiting on it, tell them the honest state: "flagged to the team, you'll hear from me when it's fixed" beats silence every time.

## What NOT to do

- Don't promise a creator an outcome that needs an operator (a payout amount, an exception, a deadline extension) before the thread confirms it
- Don't handle money or identity changes in DMs, even when you're sure
- Don't sit on something that smells wrong because it might be nothing; cheap to check, expensive to miss
