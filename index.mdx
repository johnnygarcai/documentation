## The short version of the program

Creators apply on the landing page, build an AI character in the Cantina app, and get paid for posting it to TikTok and Instagram. The program runs in three stages: **Get Set Up** (first week: setup, first character, first posts, \$40 bonus), **Bonus Ramp** (weeks 2 to 4: weekly posting bounties plus a curriculum bonus), and the **Official Program** (ongoing per-post pay). Creators move through all three on one account.

Your daily surfaces as an ambassador are the **admin dashboard** (funnel, rosters, worklists), the **sprint room** (where creators get help), and the **review queue** (posts count toward bonuses once approved).
