**This page is the running record of what's new**, with the portal front and center: every release that changes what creators see, what the program pays, or how the dashboard reads, in ambassador language rather than commit messages.

**How it works:** new entries land as things ship, written in plain language with screenshots where they help, newest on top. Anything you read here is live in the product, and a quiet stretch means nothing user-facing shipped in it.

**How to read an entry:** the label is the ship date, the subtitle is the theme, and bold leads name each change. When a change alters what you should DO (a new worklist, a moved button, a new rule), the entry says so explicitly.

<Update label="August 15, 2026" description="Graduate rows cover the whole room move, real payout totals in the Social tab, steadier forum threads">
  **Graduate rows on the dashboard now carry both room checkoffs.** Creators who finish Get Set Up leave the Sprint Room as well as join the official-program room, but the graduate worklist only tracked the invite, so the sprint-room half of the move was easy to lose track of. Each graduate row now shows two buttons, Remove and Add, and the row only reads as done once both are checked. The remove-from-room list is unchanged and still lists non-completers only, so nobody appears on it twice. The habit to build: work a graduate row all the way across, remove them from the Sprint Room in Cantina, invite them to the official room, then check off both.

  **Worklist usernames stay put after a checkoff.** With two buttons on a row, the name could get squeezed down to nothing once the row was checked, leaving nothing to click to open the profile panel. Names now hold their width and the email beside them shrinks instead, and the checked-off-by chip appears once the whole row is finished.

  **The Social tab's program split reports what was actually paid.** The Official and Sprint filters were still matching only the two retired v3 payout tracks, so they read PAID OUT \$0 and CPM \$0.00 while per-post pay was going out normally. Both sides now count the full v4 track set: the Official filter picks up \$192.50 of per-post pay it had been hiding, and the Sprint filter picks up \$600 of paid Get Set Up bonuses. If you quoted spend or CPM off that tab this week, it's worth a second look.

  **Forum threads in the portal stopped breaking on their own timestamps.** A thread's "8h ago" stamp was worked out twice, once on the server and once in the browser, and the small disagreement between the two could stop a thread card from finishing its load. The stamp is now allowed to differ quietly, so threads and replies render normally.
</Update>

<Update label="August 14, 2026" description="Two new stage emails: Get Set Up completion and the Official Program welcome">
  **Finishing Get Set Up now sends a confirmation email.** Until now the program recorded a completion and said nothing about it, so the only way to learn the sprint had passed was to open the portal and notice. The email goes out once, at the exact moment completion is recorded, and it cannot fire twice. The wording depends on the cohort: v4 creators are handed straight to the Bonus Ramp with a link into the portal and no dollar figure, since their pay is per post rather than a flat completion payout, while cohorts 1 through 5 keep the graduate copy pointing at their completion. Worth knowing for tickets: completion is recorded whether or not the email can go out, so an account that opted out of email, is blocked, or has bounced or filed a spam complaint still completes normally and simply doesn't get the note.

  **Rolling off the Bonus Ramp now sends an Official Program welcome.** This is the third and last stage email, after the Get Set Up welcome and the completion note above. An hourly job picks up v4 creators the hour they reach 28 days past the moment their setup was finished, which is the end of the three-week Bonus Ramp, and mails them once. The copy states the real Official terms: \$2.50 for every qualifying post over 350 views, up to 3 posts per day per platform on rolling 7-day periods, no weekly posting floor and no bounty to chase, and Tier 2 at \$3.50 a post for top performers. It congratulates the work rather than announcing a decision, because nobody passes or fails the Bonus Ramp, it is a pure timer. The first run clears a backlog, since everyone already past the 28-day mark comes due at once, paced at 200 emails per run.

  **The welcome email does not flip anyone's portal to the Official view.** Applying the `official_program` tag stays the manual staff step it has always been, so the portal keeps showing the Bonus Ramp view to everyone who receives this email. That view's Stage 3 state already describes the Official terms and matches the email, so nothing contradicts. If a ticket asks why the portal looks unchanged after the welcome landed, that's expected and not a bug. The usual skip rules apply here too: blocked, opted out, offboarded, bounced, and complained addresses are left alone.
</Update>

<Update label="August 13, 2026" description="The portal names when posting opens and what makes a post count, faster creator lookup on the dashboard, forum emails">
  **The portal now names the moment posting opens, instead of showing a bare 0 of 8.** Posts published during the Days 1 to 3 warm-up have never counted toward the 8, but the portal didn't say so: the hero read 0 of 8 while the post tracker chipped those same posts as counted, so the two surfaces contradicted each other and it read like broken tracking rather than a rule. The hero pill, headline, progress caption and checklist note now name the exact local moment posting starts, the post tracker carries a warm-up banner, and a post that landed outside the window says so instead of claiming it counted. When a ticket says posts aren't counting, check the posting window first.

  **The creator drawer on the dashboard gained a Posting window block.** It shows warm-up, open or closed, the window bounds in Pacific time, and the tracked, counted, in review and denied split, so "approved but didn't count" is visible arithmetic rather than a guess.

  **The Post tracker now spells out what makes a post count, before creators film.** A new card lists the three rules behind most denials: post videos, since image-only posts and photo carousels don't count; every video needs the Cantina watermark or an outro with the logo; and the approved character has to be the star, with other approved Cantina bots welcome as supporting cast. It closes by saying plainly that denied posts count toward neither the 8 nor the pay.

  **"Workspace" is retired.** Per the 8/12 team decision the portal says "creator portal" everywhere, matching the welcome email and the docs.

  **Usernames are clickable everywhere on the dashboard.** Roster rows and every worklist now open the creator profile panel on click, so reaching someone no longer means searching for them again. The creator drawer also stops covering the page on wide screens: it pushes the content aside instead, so the list stays readable while the panel is open.

  **The community forum sends email now.** Thread authors get a note when someone replies to their post, alongside the comment-reply and mention emails that already existed, and every new post and reply also lands in the ops inbox with a link straight to the thread, subject-tagged "needs review" when it's waiting on moderation.

  **Setup finished under two different emails no longer leaves the 7-day clock unstarted.** The job that stamps setup complete matched room joins by phone or email, but matched verified socials and payout by one email alone. An account whose socials and payout sit under a different address than its room join could therefore show all four setup steps done in the portal while the clock never started and no post could count. Those accounts can now have setup backdated to what the evidence shows, the latest of room join, TikTok and Instagram verification, and payout linked, which starts the window where it should have started and makes the posts countable. If a checklist reads fully complete but nothing is counting, that mismatch is worth flagging.
</Update>

<Update label="August 12, 2026" description="Posts still in review stop reading as a failed sprint, and real payout verification status">
  **The portal no longer reports a failed sprint while posts are still in our review queue.** When a Get Set Up window closed with posts still awaiting review, the earnings card read "\$0 · Not completed" and "Sprint requirements not met" and hid the payout method. That's our queue, not a failed sprint: posts count by when they were published, so one approved later still counts, and it can still flip the sprint to a pass and pay out. The card now shows how many posts are awaiting our review and that approval can complete the sprint, and the payout method stays reachable so a passing sprint never lands without a wallet linked. When a ticket comes in about a surprise zero, check first whether the posts are sitting in our review queue.

  **Approving a payout is now blocked while posts from that period are still in review.** On Get Set Up and Bonus Ramp, a payout row freezes once it's approved, so a post approved afterward lost its \$2.50 for good and could cost the \$20 bounty. The dashboard now stops that approval and says why, with a separate "Approve anyway" button for the cases that genuinely need to go through, which gets recorded. The habit to build: clear the review queue first, then approve.

  **"Check KYC" on the creator detail panel shows real payout verification status.** Until now the system stored that a payout account existed and nothing about whether identity verification actually passed, so every "it won't accept my address" ticket got answered with a guess. The panel now pulls the live status on demand, along with the attempt count, the country, and a resume link that's safe to pass along so verification can resume where it left off.

  **The portal's green payout checkmark is not proof verification passed.** Payout setup is marked complete the moment the payout widget is opened, so a verification that was later rejected still shows a green check. That hasn't changed yet, and the new panel is now where the real status lives. One caution when you use it: the blocker text in the red box comes straight from the payment provider and is written for operators, so it can read like an accusation. Explain what needs fixing in your own words rather than pasting it.
</Update>

<Update label="August 10, 2026" description="Portal lockout fixed, the \$40 bonus pays itself, clearer program terms">
  **A rare portal lockout is fixed.** When a blocked Cantina account was deleted and a new one was created on the same email, the portal broke on every load. That path is now self-healing: the portal always reads the freshest linked account, so a fresh start never gets stuck behind an abandoned one.

  **The \$40 Get Set Up bonus now pays on its own.** It used to need an amby to click a button per creator once someone qualified, and that worklist had quietly let \$480 sit unpaid. The \$40 now lands as a pending payout automatically, the same way every other earning does, and the dashboard just shows where it stands in the queue.

  **Program terms are clearer in a few places.** The pay table now shows the 8-post floor as what it is, a bonus condition with no view minimum, separate from the per-post pay rule. The FAQ spells out the four Get Set Up steps that start the 7-day clock instead of pointing elsewhere, and confirms posts are tracked automatically, no manual submission needed.
</Update>

<Update label="August 9, 2026" description="Payout widget escape hatch, blocking from the creator modal">
  **The payout widget's mobile fix got a fallback.** Even with the frame that only grows and never shrinks, the embedded form could still leave KYC fields out of reach on some devices. The widget now also offers an "Open it full screen" link, same session and same progress, just handed off to the browser's own scrolling instead of the iframe's.

  **Block and Unblock now work from the creator detail modal.** Search is how ambys usually reach a creator, but a cross-cohort search match doesn't have a roster row, so there was no way to block someone found that way. The modal now carries the same Block/Unblock control the roster tables have.
</Update>

<Update label="August 8, 2026" description="Payout form fixed on mobile, welcome email polish">
  **The payout widget's KYC step is reachable on mobile now.** The embedded payout form could report a short height on an early step, then need much more room once someone reached identity verification, and the frame wasn't growing to match, clipping the form below the fold with no way to scroll down to it. The frame now only grows and never shrinks, so the form is never cut off partway through.

  **The welcome email's setup step now says "creator portal."** Step 3 of the v4 welcome email, its button, and the plain-text version used to point to a "workspace." They now say "creator portal," matching where the link actually goes.
</Update>

<Update label="August 7, 2026" description="Faster, more accurate identity matching">
  **Account matching now runs hourly instead of once a day.** Room joins and app downloads used to take up to a full day to show up anywhere on the dashboard. The sync behind that now runs every hour, so Cantina identities show up on the roster within the hour instead of the next morning.

  **The roster now tells "pending" apart from "not linked."** An account that had been matched but had no username picked yet used to read exactly like no account at all, both showing "not linked yet." Those rows now read "username pending," so ambys can tell at a glance where the only missing step is choosing a handle.

  **Outreach lists got more accurate.** Matched accounts with no username yet were sometimes miscategorized as never having downloaded the app. They now correctly land in the room-outreach list instead of the download-outreach list, so the wrong "go download the app" nudge never goes out.
</Update>

<Update label="August 6, 2026" description="A gentler way to offboard">
  **Blocking isn't the only way to take someone out of the program anymore.** It used to be: block a creator and their whole post history got dropped from the numbers retroactively, rewriting lifetime views and CPM, even for people who simply moved on in good standing. A new `left_program` tag (applied from the same tag controls staff already use) offboards without the rewrite: tracking pauses on their existing posts, their handles come out of discovery, and payouts stop, but their post history stays put in the dashboards. Blocking still exists and still means what it always meant, for the ineligible and fraud cases that need the harsher treatment.

  **Bot accounts are off the remove-from-room worklist.** A handful of character and bot accounts (no real email, no activity, just a placeholder ID) had been slipping into our creator data and landing on the remove-from-sprint-room worklist alongside real people. That's fixed at the source now, and the handful that had already slipped through were cleaned out.

  **The pre-join screen now speaks v4.** Before Cantina data first syncs, the one screen the portal shows was still reciting the retired 14-day sprint, saying that joining the room starts a 14-day clock, even though everyone seeing it is enrolled in the current v4 program. It now shows the real v4 terms: four setup steps, a 7-day window that starts once setup is done, and the \$40 bonus at the 8-post floor. This was the largest group ever affected by stale copy, roughly 950 not-yet-joined creators across cohorts 6 through 8r, so it's a meaningful fix. The footer's copyright year got straightened out on the same pass.

  **The applicants roster shows live progress now.** Room joins only reach the dashboard once a day, so room activity could run a full day ahead of what the roster showed. Each row now carries chips for socials verification, payout account, and post count, pulled from our own data in real time, so rows that haven't synced yet are never a blank space.
</Update>
