**This page is the running record of what's new**, with the portal front and center: every release that changes what creators see, what the program pays, or how the dashboard reads, in ambassador language rather than commit messages.

**How it works:** new entries land as things ship, written in plain language with screenshots where they help, newest on top. Anything you read here is live in the product, and a quiet stretch means nothing user-facing shipped in it.

**How to read an entry:** the label is the ship date, the subtitle is the theme, and bold leads name each change. When a change alters what you should DO (a new worklist, a moved button, a new rule), the entry says so explicitly.

<Update label="August 7, 2026" description="Faster, more accurate identity matching">
  **Account matching now runs hourly instead of once a day.** Room joins and app downloads used to take up to a full day to show up anywhere on the dashboard. The sync behind that now runs every hour, so a creator's Cantina identity shows up on the roster within the hour instead of the next morning.

  **The roster now tells "pending" apart from "not linked."** A creator whose account had been matched but hadn't picked a username yet used to read exactly like someone with no account at all, both showing "not linked yet." That row now reads "username pending," so ambys can tell at a glance who just needs a nudge to finish choosing a handle.

  **Outreach lists got more accurate.** Creators with a matched account but no username yet were sometimes miscategorized as never having downloaded the app. They now correctly land in the room-outreach list instead of the download-outreach list, so nobody gets a wrong "go download the app" nudge.
</Update>

<Update label="August 6, 2026" description="A gentler way to offboard">
  **Blocking isn't the only way to take someone out of the program anymore.** It used to be: block a creator and their whole post history got dropped from the numbers retroactively, rewriting lifetime views and CPM, even for people who simply moved on in good standing. A new `left_program` tag (applied from the same tag controls staff already use) offboards without the rewrite: tracking pauses on their existing posts, their handles come out of discovery, and payouts stop, but their post history stays put in the dashboards. Blocking still exists and still means what it always meant, for the ineligible and fraud cases that need the harsher treatment.

  **Bot accounts are off the remove-from-room worklist.** A handful of character and bot accounts (no real email, no activity, just a placeholder ID) had been slipping into our creator data and landing on the remove-from-sprint-room worklist alongside real people. That's fixed at the source now, and the handful that had already slipped through were cleaned out.

  **The pre-join screen now speaks v4.** Before a creator's Cantina data first syncs, the one screen they see was still reciting the retired 14-day sprint, telling them that joining the room starts a 14-day clock, even though they're enrolled in the current v4 program. It now shows the real v4 terms: four setup steps, a 7-day window that starts once setup is done, and the \$40 bonus at the 8-post floor. This was the largest group ever affected by stale copy, roughly 950 not-yet-joined creators across cohorts 6 through 8r, so it's a meaningful fix. The footer's copyright year got straightened out on the same pass.

  **The applicants roster shows live progress now.** Room joins only reach the dashboard once a day, so a creator could already be active in the room with nothing to show for it on the roster yet. Each row now carries chips for socials verification, payout account, and post count, pulled from our own data in real time, so creators who haven't synced yet are never a blank space.
</Update>

<Update label="August 5, 2026" description="The docs hub and the portal deep-dive">
  **Room worklists follow one clear rule now.** Remove-from-room lists everyone whose Get Set Up window closed without completing it (previously only confirmed final failures showed, so expired creators sat in the room unnoticed for days), and completers land on the add-to-official-room list the moment they finish. Work the two lists and the rooms stay true.

  **This site launched.** The ambassador hub replaced the old staff handbook: `joincharacterstudio.com/docs` now brings you here. Program guide, cohort lineup, dashboard guide, daily ops, FAQ, and this changelog, behind the site login.

  **The Portal section.** Why the portal exists, every portal state with fresh screenshots, the helping-creators playbook, and the Slack request guide, because the portal is the heart of how ambassadors support the program.

  **Cantina branding** landed on the site: the wordmark, the teal, the dark theme.

  **The lander now says Character Studio.** The hero badge, FAQ copy, and page metadata (browser tab and link previews) on the public program page were renamed from Creator Studio Program to Character Studio Program, matching the name everywhere else.
</Update>

  **The entire waitlist joined the program.** All 485 eligible waitlisted people (the weekend's 279 plus 206 from the June and July rounds) were enrolled together and sent the standard welcome. 145 of them engaged the same afternoon, with the first room joins inside three hours.

  **Applicants by day.** A new chart at the top of the dashboard: new enrollments, reactivated waitlisters, and waitlist history stacked per day, scoped to the selected cohort, with the enrollment state pinned beside it.
