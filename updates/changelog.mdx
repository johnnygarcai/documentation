<Update label="August 4, 2026" description="The reactivation release">
  The biggest single day of the program so far.

  **Enrollment caps removed.** A fixed cap had quietly filled over the weekend and routed three days of applicants to the waitlist, which also silenced the ad platforms' conversion signal. Caps are gone entirely: every application now enrolls immediately, always.

  **The entire waitlist joined the program.** All 485 eligible waitlisted people (the weekend's 279 plus 206 from the June and July rounds) were enrolled together and sent the standard welcome. 145 of them engaged the same afternoon, with the first room joins inside three hours.

  **Cohort 8R.** The reactivated group lives in its own parallel cohort so cohort 8's ad-test numbers stay clean, with its own full funnel in the cohort dropdown.

  **Applicants by day.** A new chart at the top of the dashboard: new enrollments, reactivated waitlisters, and waitlist history stacked per day, scoped to the selected cohort, with the enrollment state pinned beside it.

  <Frame caption="The new applicants-by-day chart, with the reactivation visible in violet">

  </Frame>

  **Posting activity strip.** Under the funnel: creators posting, posts pending review, and posts countable now, so a young cohort in its warm-up days never reads as dead again.

  <Frame caption="Posting activity behind the posts stage">
    ![Posting activity strip](https://intent-tortoise-231.convex.cloud/api/storage/82d74e42-9cc4-43c2-ad9e-33992a14122f)
  </Frame>

  **Reactivation tracking.** The waitlist panel now shows how each reactivated person came back (batch, claim email, or self-serve), when (first real signal, timestamped), and a running came-back count.

  **Overview tiles** gained the reactivated split, and the roster got polish: real applied dates for batch-enrolled folks and single-line status pills.

  **Also that day:** the staff handbook went live at `/docs` (every internal surface documented with 24 screenshots), and the Character Curriculum was single-sourced so the coming 7-video re-cut is a one-line swap with everyone's earned bonus preserved.
</Update>
