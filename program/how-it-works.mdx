The Character Studio creator program pays people to build an AI character in the Cantina app and post it to TikTok and Instagram. One application, one account, three stages.

## The three stages

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get Set Up (first week)">
    The creator builds their character, links two brand-new social accounts, sets up payouts, and posts. Their 7-day clock starts when setup is done, never at signup. Days 1 to 3 are warm-up (follow and engage, no posting requirement); posting opens on day 4. Hitting the 8-post floor earns a $$40 bonus** with no view minimum, and every qualifying post (200+ views) pays **$$**2.50** on top, up to 24 posts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bonus Ramp (weeks 2 to 4)">
    Up to $$80 more** on top of posting pay: three **$$**20 weekly posting bounties** (10 posts a week, 5 per platform, at least one post over 200 views) plus a one-time **\$20 curriculum bonus** for finishing the Character Curriculum videos in the first 30 days. Missing a week only costs that week's bounty; the next one opens as normal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Official Program (ongoing)">
    Flat **\$2.50 per qualifying post** (the view threshold rises to 350), plus extra pay when a post takes off. No cap and no ladder, paid on a rolling 7-day basis for as long as they keep creating. To stay active, creators post at least once every 7 days.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Cohorts

Creators are grouped into cohorts, and almost every number on the dashboard is scoped to one. Two kinds:

- **Launch cohorts** are date-chained: every new signup after the cohort's start date lands there, until the next cohort opens. Cohort 8 (started July 31) is the current one.
- **Audience tests** are parallel cohorts assigned by link or by staff, running alongside a launch. They keep experiments separate: the 18-to-30 ads test, the creators-community test, and most recently **Cohort 8R**, which holds the 485 waitlisted people we brought into the program on August 4.

<Warning>
  Cohorts run different program versions with genuinely different requirements. Cohorts 1 through 5 ran the earlier 14-day sprint formats; cohorts 6 through 8R run the current three-stage program. Comparing stage counts across versions is comparing different things.
</Warning>

## Enrollment

Enrollment is **open with no cap** (as of August 4). Every application enrolls immediately and the creator gets the welcome email with their portal sign-in. Earlier in the program a fixed enrollment cap silently filled and routed three days of applicants to a waitlist; the cap is gone for good and the dashboard now shows enrollment state at a glance.
