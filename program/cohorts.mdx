Every creator belongs to a cohort, and each cohort is an experiment with its own funnel on the dashboard. This is the current lineup and how to read it.

## The lineup

| Cohort | Started | Type | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Cohort 8R** | Aug 4 | Audience test | The reactivated waitlist: 485 people enrolled together the day caps were removed |
| **Cohort 8** | Jul 31 | Launch (current) | The checklist \+ explainer-video layout, on broad ads. New signups land here |
| **Cohort 7** | Jul 24 | Launch | First public run of the three-stage program, before the checklist returned |
| **Cohort 6** | Jul 27 | Audience test | Invite-only seed group that tested the three-stage program first |
| **Cohorts 1 to 5** | Jun 10 to Jul 15 | Earlier eras | The original 14-day sprint formats (\$150 completion payout) and its audience tests |

<Warning>
  Cohorts 1 to 5 ran different requirements than 6 through 8R. The dashboard shows each cohort its own stages, so never compare stage counts across the two eras directly.
</Warning>

## The three live experiments

**Cohort 7 vs Cohort 8** is the layout test: cohort 7 launched without the portal's clickable checklist, cohort 8 launched with it plus explainer videos. Watching where each cohort's creators stall tells us which onboarding actually works.

**Cohort 8** is also the scale read: it's the biggest cohort so far on a broad ad campaign, so its stage conversions are the best signal of how the program performs beyond hand-picked audiences.

Their chart tells the story: amber days while they queued, then the violet bar when the gates opened. 145 of 485 engaged the same afternoon.
