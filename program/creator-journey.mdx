When a creator asks a question, it helps to see what they're looking at. This is their journey in order, and the goal of the preview system behind it is bigger than screenshots: **you can support a creator from any cohort by pulling up exactly what they saw**, because the previews are grouped by program version, step by step through their funnel. Every screen here is walkable with mock data on the [live previews page](https://www.joincharacterstudio.com/previews), no login needed, and [the portal tour](/portal/tour#any-cohort-any-era) maps which view matches which cohort.

## 1. The landing page

They arrive from an ad or a shared link at `joincharacterstudio.com/earn`. The page explains the three stages and the pay, and the application is just phone \+ email. Attribution from the link they clicked rides along with the signup.

<Frame caption="The program landing page">
  ![Landing page](https://www.joincharacterstudio.com/docs/creator-landing.png)
</Frame>

## 2. The welcome email and onboarding

Enrolling triggers the welcome email with their portal link. First portal visit shows a short click-through explaining the program before the workspace opens.

<Frame caption="The onboarding walkthrough, mobile-first">
  ![Onboarding walkthrough](https://www.joincharacterstudio.com/docs/creator-onboarding.png)
</Frame>

## 3. The portal

Their workspace: a task checklist, linked accounts, payouts, the Learn tab (Character Curriculum), community, and support. What they see depends on their stage; graduates get the Official view with its own pay structure.

<Frame caption="The creator portal (preview mode, mock data)">
  ![Creator portal](https://www.joincharacterstudio.com/docs/creator-portal.png)
</Frame>

## 4. Posting and getting paid

Posts from their linked accounts are tracked automatically and reviewed by the team before counting toward bonuses. Earnings accumulate in the portal, and payouts go through the payout method they set up (see [How payouts work](/program/payouts)).

<Tip>
  On a support question, open the same preview state the creator is describing. The dashboard's **Site previews** tab has every portal state (fresh signup, mid-posting, bonus unlocked, Official) as one-click links.
</Tip>
