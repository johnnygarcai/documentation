The portal at `joincharacterstudio.com/portal` is the creator's home base for everything the Cantina app can't do: tracking their progress, proving they own their social accounts, learning the craft, and getting paid. Understanding _why_ it exists makes almost every ambassador conversation easier.

## The two-surface problem

A creator's life in this program is split across two surfaces:

- **The Cantina app** is where the creative work happens: building the character, joining the sprint room, being part of the community.
- **The portal** is where the _program_ happens: the checklist, account verification, post tracking, the curriculum, earnings, and payouts.

That split is the single biggest source of creator confusion. Someone can be highly active in the app and still miss their bonus because they never came back to the portal to link accounts or set up payouts. A big part of the ambassador job is bridging that gap: when a creator seems lost, the answer is almost always "open your portal."

<Note>
  The simplest way to explain it to a creator: **the app is where you create, the portal is where you get paid.** Money is the one thing that only lives in the portal, and every requirement on the checklist is a step on the way to it.
</Note>

## What the portal is designed to do

**A creator should never have to ask where they stand.** That's the design goal behind every section. The portal shows, live and automatically:

- Which stage they're in and what their next move is (the stage banner and "your next move" card)
- Exactly which setup steps and requirements are done, in progress, or missing
- Every post we've tracked, with its views and engagement
- What they've earned, what's confirmed, and where it will be sent

Creators never upload proof of anything. Posts are tracked automatically from their verified accounts, in-app activity flows in from Cantina, and the checklist fills itself in. When a creator says "I did the thing but it doesn't show," the portal is where you look together.

## Why we keep pulling creators back to it

Creators live in the app and on TikTok; they don't naturally return to a website. But the portal visit is where activation actually happens: linking accounts, setting up payouts, watching the curriculum. So the program is built to create reasons to return:

- The welcome email and every reminder deep-links into the portal
- Sign-in is a one-time email code, so there's no password to lose
- The \$40 setup bonus requires portal steps, which front-loads the habit
- The curriculum (and its \$20 bonus) lives in the portal's Learn tab

When you nudge a creator in the room or over DM, the most useful single link you can send is their portal.

## The portal changes with the creator

The same URL renders differently depending on where someone is: a fresh signup sees the setup checklist front and center, a mid-week poster sees their posting progress and bonus tracker, and a graduate sees the Official Program view with its own pay structure. The [portal tour](/portal/tour) walks every state with screenshots.
