Most creator questions are one of about eight situations, and each maps to a specific portal section plus a specific dashboard check. This is the playbook.

## "I can't sign in"

The portal uses a one-time email code, no password. The three usual causes: they're checking a different inbox than the one they applied with (the code goes to their application email), the code expired (they can request a fresh one), or the email landed in spam. If they genuinely can't access that inbox anymore, escalate through Slack: changing a creator's email touches their whole progress record.

## "My clock started late / I didn't know it started"

The 7-day clock starts when the LAST setup step is checked, never at signup. The Tasks tab says this on the fresh state. If a creator believes their clock is wrong, check their roster row on the dashboard: it shows the exact setup-completion timestamp their window is anchored to.

## "I linked my TikTok but it doesn't show"

Linked Accounts works by bio-code: they enter a handle, we issue a short code, they paste it in their bio, and we check. The classic failure is the handle being typed with different capitalization or the bio not saved before verifying. Have them re-run it in Linked Accounts; the check is on-demand and free to retry. On the dashboard, **Verification health** shows whether verification is failing program-wide (in which case it's us).

## "My posts aren't counting"

Three layers, in order:

1. **Was it posted during their countable window?** Warm-up days (1 to 3) don't count toward the floor; posting opens day 4.
2. **Is it pending review?** Posts count once approved in the review queue. This is the most common answer, and credit applies retroactively, so the honest response is "it's in review; it will count and nothing is lost."
3. **Is the account verified?** Posts are only tracked from verified handles. Unverified account means invisible posts.

The dashboard's posting activity strip shows all three layers at a glance for the whole cohort.

## "Where's my \$40 / \$20 / payout?"

Walk the [payout pipeline](/program/payouts): earned → pending → approved → sent → paid. The creator-side view is the Payouts tab; the ops-side truth is the payout ledger. The \$40 specifically is paid manually, so "confirmed in the portal" and "money moved" are separated by the ops run. Set the expectation kindly and check the worklist.

## "The curriculum says 9/10 but I watched everything"

The per-video support view on the dashboard shows exactly which video didn't credit and why (usually a player that never reported the finish). Staff can credit it by hand, and the fix is logged. Escalate with the creator's email and which video.

## "I'm in the app every day, why am I behind?"

The two-surface problem in its purest form (see [Why the portal exists](/portal/why-the-portal)). In-app activity matters, but requirements like linking accounts, payouts, and the curriculum only happen in the portal. The kindest frame: "you've done the creative half; ten minutes in your portal catches the program half up."

## "Can you just fix it for me?"

Sometimes yes: staff tools exist for crediting curriculum videos, adjusting rooms, and correcting records, all logged. The rule is that anything touching money or identity goes through Slack so an operator makes the change with an audit trail. What to include when you escalate is on [Submitting requests](/guide/slack-requests).
