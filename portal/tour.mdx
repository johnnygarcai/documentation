The portal renders differently depending on where a creator is in the program. These are the states in the order a creator moves through them, captured from the live preview system (mock data). Every state is also a one-click link on the dashboard's **Site previews** tab when you want to poke at it yourself.

The left sidebar is constant throughout: **Tasks** (the checklist and stage tracker), **Linked Accounts** (social verification), **Payouts**, **Learn** (the Character Curriculum), **Community**, **Tools** (concept tester and idea generators), and **Support**.

## Fresh signup

What a creator sees on their very first visit: the explainer video, the three-stage banner with "you are here," and the setup checklist at zero. The \$40 tracker on the right shows what's waiting once the four setup steps and the posting floor are done. Their 7-day clock has NOT started; it only starts when the last setup step is checked, and the page says so.

## Payout setup

The Payouts tab mid-setup. This is one of the four setup steps and the one creators skip most, because it feels optional until money is owed. It isn't optional: it gates the clock start.

## Setup done, no character yet

Setup is checked off, the clock is running, and the portal pivots to the next move: build the character in the app. This is the state where creators most need a nudge back INTO the app, the one moment the portal pushes them away from itself.

## Posting days

Days 4 to 7: the posting window is open and the portal becomes a progress tracker: posts counted against the 8-post floor, views per post, and the bonus math updating live. The screenshot on [What creators see](/program/creator-journey) shows this state.

## The Learn tab

The Character Curriculum with its \$20 bonus panel and progress counter, plus supporting guides. Watching is verified server-side: a creator has to actually spend the time, and the manual "I finished this" button runs the same checks when a player misbehaves.

## The Official Program view

Graduates see a different portal: flat per-post pay, the 350-view threshold, rolling 7-day payouts, and their posting streak. The switch is controlled by a tag the team applies at graduation.

## Any cohort, any era

The states above are the current program, but creators from every cohort write in, and the most common support question is some version of "what did they see?" That's exactly what the preview system was built for: on the dashboard's **Site previews** tab, the journeys are grouped **by program version**. Select the view matching the creator's cohort and you get every step of the funnel THEY walked, in order: their landing page, their onboarding, their portal at each stage.

| Creator's cohort | Select this view | What you'll see |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 6, 7, 8, 8R | New program (v4) | The three-stage journey: lander, onboarding, week-1 workspace states, Bonus Ramp |
| 2, 3, 4, 5 | 14-Day Sprint (v2) | The sprint-era onboarding and portal they signed up under |
| 1 | 14-Day Sprint (v1) | The original portal |
| Graduates | Official Program | The Official view with its pay structure |

So when a cohort 2 creator asks why their portal mentioned a \$150 payout and a cohort 8 creator asks about the \$40 bonus, you can pull up each one's actual screens and answer from what they genuinely saw rather than from memory.

<Tip>
  Preview links accept a state parameter, so you can open any of these directly. On a support call, open the state the creator is in and you're looking at the same screen they are, minus their personal data.
</Tip>
